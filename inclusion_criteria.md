Include only if all of these are true:

1. The patients are humans with a cartilage or osteochondral defect in the knee. Ankle, hip and other joints do not count.
2. The study compares two or more treatments against each other. Examples are microfracture, chondrocyte implantation, osteochondral transplant, scaffolds, and cell or stem cell therapy. A comparison of two techniques, an add-on to a treatment, or two rehabilitation plans also counts.
3. The study reports patient outcomes after follow-up, such as pain, function scores or MRI findings.
4. The study is a randomized trial or a comparative clinical study.

Exclude animal studies, laboratory studies, reviews, meta-analyses, case series with one treatment group, technique descriptions, and studies that only test an imaging method.
